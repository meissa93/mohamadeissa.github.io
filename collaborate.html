Open to Collaborations

Great things come from different minds, shared winds…

What I’m looking for

Researchers who want to provide a better understanding of non-stationary winds and what they do to our communities…

There’s plenty to work on, including, but not limited to: how to estimate their peaks, how their effects compare with those of straight-line winds, and, most importantly, how to make the most of the efforts, facilities, and research groups already out there so we can move forward together. The work is still in its infancy stage…

What keeps me excited lately

  • Estimating peak aerodynamic pressures under non-stationary winds.
  • How building aerodynamics differ between non-stationary and atmospheric boundary layer (ABL) wind fields—a comparison that has yet to be fully explored.
  • Many facilities, many studies. Let’s connect the dots, together.

Who I’d love to hear from

Students, researchers, professors, research engineers — literally anyone who shares these interests…

How to reach out

I’m open to all intriguing research ideas; all you have to do is email me or send me a LinkedIn message…
