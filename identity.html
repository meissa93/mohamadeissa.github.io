Roots & Journey

I was born in Cairo in 1993 and have been living in the US since 2022. I was fortunate enough to live, for a very short time, in Italy during my Ph.D. years…

What brought me here was a thought at 2 a.m.: should I pursue a doctoral degree, or settle for my master's as the furthest I could go? It was me talking and praying to God for guidance—I couldn't afford the regret receipt of not going all the way, yet, at the same time, I trust God's plans. Unapologetically, I emailed 300 professors—it was hard to land an opportunity if you weren't already in academia; I was just an engineer. Long story short, I had one month to get an offer to pursue my Ph.D., or accept what was written for me. I think we now know the answer to my prayers…
