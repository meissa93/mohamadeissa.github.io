Journal and conference logos (optional)
=======================================
Put a logo file here with one of the names below and it appears automatically
next to the venue on the Publications page and in Selected Publications on Home.
Each venue already has a simple placeholder badge (<name>.svg, just the
abbreviation in green). To use the real logo, save it as <name>.png (or .jpg
or .webp) - it automatically takes priority over the .svg placeholder.
To use a real SVG logo instead, overwrite <name>.svg. To show no logo at all,
delete the file(s) for that name.
Wide, short logos look best (about 3:1). They sit on a small white chip so they
stay readable in dark mode.

Journals
  jweia.*            Journal of Wind Engineering and Industrial Aerodynamics
  pem.*              Probabilistic Engineering Mechanics
  jobe.*             Journal of Building Engineering
  infrastructures.*  Infrastructures (MDPI)
  ijser.*            International Journal of Scientific & Engineering Research

  bbaa.*             Bluff Body Aerodynamics and Applications (BBAA IX)

  in-vento.*         IN-VENTO (Italian Association for Wind Engineering)
  icsge.*            International Conference on Structural and Geotechnical Engineering
  iscsee.*           International Summit on Civil, Structural, and Environmental Engineering

New venue? In the HTML add data-logo="your-name" to that venue's line and
save the logo here as your-name.png (or .svg/.jpg/.webp).
Check each publisher's or organizer's logo-use rules before publishing.
